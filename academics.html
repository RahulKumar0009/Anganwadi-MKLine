Academics

Holistic early learning programmes designed for every child's growth and development.

What We Offer

Our Learning Areas

Our curriculum focuses on the all-round development of children aged 3–6 years through play, creativity, and exploration.

📚

Early Literacy

Introducing children to letters, sounds, and stories through fun and interactive storytelling, rhymes, and reading activities.

🔢

Numeracy & Counting

Building number sense and basic math skills through games, blocks, sorting, and everyday counting activities.

🎨

Art & Creativity

Encouraging self-expression through drawing, painting, craft, and music — fostering imagination and fine motor skills.

🌿

Environmental Awareness

Teaching children about nature, plants, animals, and the environment through hands-on exploration and simple science activities.

🤝

Social & Emotional Learning

Helping children build friendships, share, cooperate, and manage their feelings in a supportive group environment.

🏃

Physical Development

Promoting gross and fine motor skills through outdoor play, exercises, dance, and engaging physical activities.

🥗

Health & Nutrition

Providing nutritious meals and teaching children about hygiene, healthy habits, and the importance of a balanced diet.

🎭

Cultural Activities

Celebrating festivals, local traditions, and cultural heritage through songs, dances, and community participation.
