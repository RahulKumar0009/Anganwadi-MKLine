Our Story

Welcome to Anganwadi MK Line

Anganwadi MK Line is a dedicated early childhood care and education centre committed to the holistic development of every child. We provide a warm, safe, and stimulating environment where children can learn, grow, and thrive.

Our centre is part of the national Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) programme, ensuring quality care, nutrition support, and early learning for all children in our community.
